# Before you show anything

Every line here is a correction Manan has actually made. Run the list on the finished piece (every slide at its last step, every screen, every shot), fix what fails, then show it.

## Taste
- [ ] **Less, but perfect.** One idea and one hero per slide/screen/shot. No confetti, sparkles, extra Milos, badge rows or random props.
- [ ] **Minimal is not empty.** Every section is still there, done once and well: a confident hero with real colour mass, 2 or 3 tones, generous space.
- [ ] Nothing crowded. If it's tight, split it across clicks or screens instead of shrinking it.
- [ ] Lines don't wrap unevenly; no orphan word on its own line.
- [ ] Copying a reference's structure is not the brief; its purpose is. Keep Aha's rules and mix in the feel. If Manan shares a reference animation to match, copy it one to one from a frame-by-frame breakdown, not from memory.

## Colour and grain
- [ ] Product UI is monochrome: no coloured buttons, chips, badges or selected states. Selection = ink border.
- [ ] Grain is two tones of one family, blended only by dots, lit top-left, with hand-drawn lines inside the form. No smooth gradients, no grey noise.
- [ ] Grain never on buttons, nav, text blocks, photos, Milo, chat, auth or payment.
- [ ] In the app: at most one grain piece per screen, for a person you're about to meet or a moment that matters.
- [ ] No per-item colours without meaning (no rainbow of provider cards).
- [ ] Text on colour passes contrast. White text on grain cards (Manan prefers it).

## Type and copy
- [ ] Fonts: Hedvig Letters Serif (headings), Satoshi (UI/body), Schoolbell (handwritten). Nothing else unless asked.
- [ ] Presentations: nothing under 26px on a 1600 × 900 stage, headings 76px+.
- [ ] No em dashes. No emojis in UI copy (chat reactions only). Plain everyday words.
- [ ] No labels, slot names or notes on screen ("A MASTERCLASS BY ...", "PLACEHOLDER"). Placeholders are blank shapes.
- [ ] Any words you invented are flagged to Manan.

## Components
- [ ] Icons are Phosphor only. Sticker icons sit on top of cards, never as tiny bullets.
- [ ] No pills around labels in decks.
- [ ] Primary button = `#FDF6F4` fill + 1px ink stroke. Back = CaretCircleLeft. Close = X.
- [ ] Provider cards: five things only (name first, years, expertise, next slot, price). Price never the biggest thing.
- [ ] No tilted cards. Nothing shakes or vibrates.

## Motion
- [ ] Shape and illustration motion is stepped at 8 to 12 fps on one shared clock.
- [ ] No bounces or springs in UI. Eases only. (Milo's hops excepted.)
- [ ] Text arrives word by word or line by line, calmly. No scale slams, no tilted pop-ins.
- [ ] Transitions are obvious and named: match cut, shape wipe, container transform, particle morph.
- [ ] Video camera pushes in on what's about to be clicked; never follows the cursor.
- [ ] One idea per click; lists appear one item at a time.

## Milo
- [ ] Milo is drawn by the shared code, not redrawn. No sharp corners. Solid eyes. Flat, no grain.
- [ ] Never on top of text. Optically centred in any shape.
- [ ] His colour means something. Pink = heart eyes.
- [ ] His lines pass the "only Milo would say this" test.

## Sound and haptics
- [ ] No sound on every tap or arrow key. Sound only where it tells the story.
- [ ] No copyrighted sound or music in anything public.
- [ ] App haptics are hard and crisp ("tak tak"), never soft buzzes.

## People and data
- [ ] No real teammates' photos, names, voice notes or real provider data in anything public unless Manan says so.
- [ ] No invented credentials, reviews or numbers. Placeholder data is labelled in your message to Manan (not on screen).

## Shipping
- [ ] No console errors. Built file opens offline (fonts embedded where possible).
- [ ] One stable review link, updated in place. Never hand over a new URL per change.
- [ ] Ask before the first public deploy (public or password?).
- [ ] Figma is read-only unless Manan asks. Never edit or delete his designs; write to a separate page and confirm first.
- [ ] Tell Manan what you couldn't check yourself (sound, a real phone, a projector).
